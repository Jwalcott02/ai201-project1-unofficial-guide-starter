FAU Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
Program Summary (Requires 120 credits)
Source: fau.edu/engineering/eecs/undergraduate/computer-science/program-sumary-bscs/

DEGREE OVERVIEW
Total Credits Required: 120
- General Education: 24 credits
- Mathematics: 11 credits
- Science: 7 credits
- Common Core: 24 credits
- Computer Science - Computer Engineering Core: 15 credits
- Computer Science Core: 12 credits
- Semi-Core Group 1: 6 credits
- Semi-Core Group 2: 6 credits
- Electives: 15 credits

MATHEMATICS REQUIREMENTS
- Calculus with Analytic Geometry 1 (MAC 2311) - 4 credits
- Calculus with Analytic Geometry 2 (MAC 2312) - 4 credits
- Matrix Theory (MAS 2103) - 3 credits

Certain 5000 or 6000-level courses may also count. See advisor for current list.
